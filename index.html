How to read this
  • Median is the middle value across reports, which is usually more realistic. Best is the highest number anyone reported. Switch with the “Show” menu.
  • Spot is how hard it is to find a party on the map, from 1 (very easy, every channel is empty) to 5 (very hard, over 30 minutes to find a party).
  • A ⚠ means the best report is far above the others, so treat it with caution.
  • Limited data means fewer than 3 reports.
  • Everything is a player estimate. Gear, route, party and uptime change the result.
